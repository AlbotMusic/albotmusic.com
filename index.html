Origin

From Detroit, the long way around

DetroitHumble beginnings in a city known for building things by hand.
Middle schoolFirst love of music. It became the thing that made sense.
High schoolKept playing, kept creating — a habit more than a plan.
2020The pandemic rekindled it. Albot Music LLC and the artist Albot Music™ were born.

Albot Music started with humble beginnings in Detroit — a kid in a middle school music room who found something worth holding onto. That love of music carried all the way through high school.

Then life moved on, and the music went quiet for years. It took the stillness of the COVID-19 pandemic to bring it back — this time for good. That's when Albot Music LLC was founded and the artist Albot Music™ started releasing.

Every track is instrumental and self-made: written, played, and produced independently, from the first idea to the final mix.

The one exception

Love Requited

The only song in the catalog with vocals — my wife and I professing our love for one another. An instrumental version is available too.
